### About

AI Developer and Robotics Engineer with hands-on experience in **LLM fine-tuning** (T5, Gemma, LLaMA with LoRA, QLoRA, and RAG) and **real-time robotic systems** using ROS 2 and Jetson platforms. Proven track record in applied research, publishing papers, and building intelligent systems — from autonomous vehicles and IoT to drone and robotic arm development.

- Currently **Physical AI Engineer** at [orinu.ai](https://orinu.ai/) — LLM fine-tuning, Smart Braille Printer, robotic arm (ROS 2)
- Previously **AI & IoT Researcher** at Kookmin University under Prof. Jang Yeong Min
- Published in **IEEE Access**, **Sensors**, **ICAIIC**, and **RoboNation**
- Contributed to **8 government-funded research projects** (MOTIE, MSIT, MSS)
- Open to freelance AI/robotics engineering & research consulting

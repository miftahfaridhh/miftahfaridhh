### About

I'm a researcher working at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **IoT**, and **communications engineering**. My work focuses on deep learning for Optical Camera Communication (OCC), smart energy forecasting, and 5G/V2X systems.

- Conducted research under **Prof. Jang Yeong Min** at Kookmin University
- Contributed to **8 government-funded research projects** (MOTIE, MSIT, MSS)
- Published in **IEEE Access**, **Sensors**, and **ICAIIC**
- Currently at [**OrinuAI**](https://orinu.ai/) — open to freelance research & engineering projects
